# SmartBudgetManager
A project for managing budget
SmartBudget Manager
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook (`SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`) that helps you track your personal budget. You type your income and expenses; the workbook calculates your monthly balance, shows your spending by category in a chart, and warns you when you go over budget.

Version 1.0 – October 2026 – Prepared by NameOfStudent – Group (ESI-SBA, TP 1)

What it does
Records monthly income and expenses
Sorts expenses into categories: Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education
Calculates total income, total expenses and the monthly balance automatically
Displays a pie chart "Expenses by Category"
Shows WITHIN BUDGET (green) or OVER BUDGET (red) against your overall monthly budget
What you need
Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible spreadsheet program)
The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx (no macros, no internet connection, no add-in)
Basic Excel skills: open a file, click a cell, type a number
Get started in 5 steps
1.
Open SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. If a yellow bar says Protected View, click Enable Editing.
2.
Go to the Categories sheet and set the Monthly Budget of each category.
3.
Go to the Transactions sheet. In the first empty row, enter the Date and a Description.
4.
Choose the Type (Income or Expense) and, for an expense, the Category from the drop-down lists. Type the amount in the Income or Expense column.
5.
Open the Dashboard sheet to read your totals, balance, chart and budget status.
The three worksheets
Worksheet	Use
Dashboard	Read totals, balance, budget status and chart (nothing to type)
Transactions	Enter income and expenses, one per row (rows 4 to 50)
Categories	See categories and change the monthly budget of each one
Good to know
Do not type over the Month column or over cells with totals; they are automatic.
Use Income OR Expense on a row, never both. Always choose a Category for an expense, or it will be missing from the chart.
Only the first 5 categories and rows 4–50 are counted on the Dashboard.
Totals cover all rows. For a new month, save a copy (for example `SmartBudget_2026-11.xlsx`) and clear the old transactions.
The red warning compares total expenses with the overall budget, not each category separately.
